Good Arrows privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Good Arrows is a darts scoring app published by Ludis Mgmt Ltd ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with information.

What stays on your device

The player names you type, your scores, your settings and your scoreboard of finished games are stored on your device only. We do not receive them and we cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Accounts, ads and tracking

The app has no accounts. It shows no adverts, includes no analytics, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Subscriptions

If you buy Good Arrows Pro, as a subscription or a one-off purchase, the purchase is handled by Apple through your Apple Account. We never see your payment details.

We use RevenueCat to check whether your purchase is active. RevenueCat receives a random identifier for your installation of the app, your purchase history for this app (product, purchase and renewal dates, and the App Store country and currency), and basic technical details such as the app version and device type. It does not receive your name, email address or player names. RevenueCat's privacy policy is at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above.
